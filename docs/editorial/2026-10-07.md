## Saints Peter and Paul Church closes temporarily after roof problem

Section: local · Community: Sandusky · Byline: JD

Summary: Weekday Masses move to St. Mary's Oct. 7–9 while the parish seeks a temporary repair. The hoped-for reopening date is not confirmed.

Saints Peter and Paul Church in Sandusky has temporarily closed after an engineering inspection found a compromised roof support, the Catholic Parishes of Sandusky said.

An engineering firm examined the church's attic roof beams Oct. 2. Nearly all were in good condition, but one was compromised, according to the [parish notice](https://sanduskycatholic.org/).

Weekday Masses scheduled at Saints Peter and Paul Oct. 7–9 will instead take place at noon at St. Mary's Church, 429 Central Ave. Confessions scheduled for 5 p.m. Thursday, Oct. 8, also move to St. Mary's.

The parish says the church will remain closed until a temporary repair can be made. It hopes to complete that work by Oct. 9; the notice does not confirm a reopening date or give a repair cost.

Pastoral offices remain open on their regular schedule. Check the parish website for updates before attending services.

## Erie County sheriff raises FBI fingerprinting fees

Section: local · Community: Erie County · Byline: JD

Summary: The office lists $39 for an FBI check and $73 for combined state and federal checks effective Oct. 1. Appointments are required.

The Erie County Sheriff's Office lists higher fees for civilian fingerprinting involving FBI background checks, effective Oct. 1.

A dated notice on the office's [fingerprinting page](https://www.eriecounty.oh.gov/CivilianFingerprinting.aspx) sets the FBI check at $39 and a combined Ohio Bureau of Criminal Investigation and FBI check at $73.

An older paragraph on the same page still lists $36 and $70, respectively. Call the office to confirm the amount before obtaining payment.

Electronic fingerprinting requires an appointment and is scheduled Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 3:45 p.m., except government holidays and the day after Thanksgiving. Call 419-625-7951, ext. 6256, to schedule.

Applicants must bring a valid government-issued photo ID, the required background-check reason code and the mailing address for results. Payment must be a certified bank check or money order payable to the Erie County Sheriff's Office; cash, credit cards and personal checks are not accepted.

The office is at 2800 Columbus Ave. in Sandusky. Civilian fingerprints are taken in the concealed handgun licensing office.

## Huron library book sale opens tonight for members; public sale starts Thursday

Section: community · Community: Huron · Byline: JD

Summary: The sale runs Oct. 7–10 at Huron Public Library, with memberships available at tonight's preview and a $5 bag sale Saturday.

Friends of Huron Public Library will open a four-day book sale Wednesday, Oct. 7, with a members-only evening before public shopping begins Thursday.

Tonight's sale runs from 4:30-7:30 p.m. in Rooms A and B at the library, 333 Williams St. Shoppers can pay for a membership at the door, according to the [library's event listing](https://huronlibrary.libcal.com/event/17518782).

Scanners are prohibited Wednesday through Friday and welcome Saturday, when shoppers can buy a bag of books for $5.
