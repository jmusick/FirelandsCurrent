## Pipe Creek parking lot closes as Sandusky begins access improvements

Section: outdoors · Community: Sandusky · Byline: JD

Summary: The $58,071.75 project improves access near the former rowing association building and kayak launch. Construction is scheduled to finish Dec. 1.

The parking lot at Pipe Creek Wildlife Area is closed while Sandusky improves access around the former rowing association building and kayak launch.

The city reported the closure in its [Oct. 8 announcement](https://www.facebook.com/cityofsandusky/posts/pfbid0hTuHhdTB5Ha1DqkByczouKCJEM7hHhqGAagkduamhp2eoNLve6YX1j15eyf9LHZel). Its [project page](https://www.cityofsandusky.com/city_projects/pipe_creek_parking_lot.php) says construction began in early October and is scheduled to finish Dec. 1.

Underground Solutions of Sandusky received the $58,071.75 contract, funded through the city's Capital Projects Fund.

Plans call for gravel improvements, a wider northern entrance, stone pathways and a loop south of the building to improve access to the kayak and canoe launch. Crews will clear vegetation within about 15 feet of the building and prune tree limbs where needed.

The city says some trees will be removed where necessary, while larger mature trees will be preserved.

Sandusky maintains the wildlife area's entrance; the Ohio Department of Natural Resources manages its wetland cells. The city acquired the former Sandusky Bay Rowing Association storage building in 2025 and has used it for kayak storage and programs with local organizations.

The city warns of equipment activity and limited access during construction. The Dec. 1 date is the scheduled completion, not a confirmed parking-lot reopening. Check the project page for updates before visiting.

## Sandusky Halloween decorating contest entries due Oct. 16

Section: community · Community: Sandusky · Byline: JD

Summary: Homes within city limits can enter four categories. Public voting opens the week of Oct. 19, and the entry form requires a photo and permission to display it.

Sandusky residents have until Friday, Oct. 16, to enter their homes in the city's Haunt the House Halloween decorating contest.

The city is organizing the contest with Greater Sandusky Partnership and Destination Sandusky, according to its [Oct. 8 announcement](https://www.facebook.com/cityofsandusky/posts/pfbid0apAwdojC59ZcqtMx8ff2p1mwALmq5w7rZcvbL7WhskKkD1jgvscmdhcyzhCFCHhZl).

The four categories are spookiest, traditional fall, funniest and people's choice. Public voting will open the week of Oct. 19. Homes must be within Sandusky city limits.

The city's [entry form](https://www.cityofsandusky.com/HauntedHouse) asks for a name, email address, phone number, display address and photo. Entrants must confirm they took the photo or have permission to share it, and allow the city and partnership to use it in the public voting survey and their communications.

The form also allows someone to nominate another person's house and asks for the owner's contact information in that case.

The city says winners will receive a prize. Its announcement does not specify the prizes, a deadline time or when winners will be named.

## Sandusky students to present art, spooky stories and music Monday

Section: schools · Community: Sandusky · Byline: JD

Summary: The Oct. 12 programs at the Regional Center for Arts & Academic Studies begin with an art show at 4:30 p.m., followed by a gifted student showcase at 6 p.m.

Sandusky students will present a fear-themed art show and a showcase of stories, drama and music Monday, Oct. 12, at the Regional Center for Arts & Academic Studies.

Both programs take place at 125 E. Adams St.

The MZ Student Art Gallery's [“Phobia...the Fear of...” show](https://www.scs-k12.net/protected/ArticleView.aspx?dasi=3GGI&iid=6IIGAU2) runs from 4:30-6 p.m. The district's Oct. 7 deadline to submit artwork has passed.

At 6 p.m., the center will host its Fall Gifted Family Focus Workshop and Showcase, titled [“Frights, Fate, and Frets.”](https://www.scs-k12.net/protected/ArticleView.aspx?dasi=3GGI&iid=6IIG0BB) Fifth and sixth graders will perform spooky stories, Greek tragedy and live guitar music, according to the district's announcement.

The notices do not list admission charges or registration requirements. Families seeking those details can contact Sandusky City Schools at 419-626-6940.
