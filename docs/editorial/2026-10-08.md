## Port Clinton cleanup is Saturday; residents must unload their own vehicles

Section: local · Community: Port Clinton · Byline: JD

Summary: The Oct. 10 drop-off runs from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the city-designated City Hall parking lot. Proof of city residency is required, and several materials are excluded.

Port Clinton residents can bring discarded items to the city's fall cleanup Saturday, Oct. 10, but must unload their own vehicles and bring proof of residency.

The drop-off runs from 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the City Hall parking lot identified in the [city's announcement](https://www.facebook.com/photo/?fbid=1536731205167226&set=a.233506642156362). Drivers should enter through the Police Station entrance off Buckeye Boulevard.

City crews will operate heavy equipment to move discarded items into dumpsters. They will not remove items from residents' vehicles or trailers, the notice says.

The collection is limited to city residents. The city does not specify in the notice which documents it accepts as proof of residency.

Excluded items are hazardous materials, lawn and landscaping materials, contractor construction materials, tires, paint, computers, televisions, air conditioners and refrigerators.

The notice does not list a disposal fee. Residents with questions about eligibility or what they can bring can call 419-734-5522, ext. 239, or email pcadmin@portclinton.com.

## Oak Harbor Apple Festival to bring parade, car show and races this weekend

Section: community · Community: Oak Harbor · Byline: JD

Summary: The Oct. 10–11 festival has no admission fee. Saturday's parade starts at 2 p.m.; Sunday's car show and races take place at separate locations.

The Oak Harbor Apple Festival returns downtown Saturday and Sunday, Oct. 10–11, with a parade, vendors, entertainment and Sunday races.

The [Oak Harbor Area Chamber of Commerce](https://www.oakharborohio.net/partnership/apple-festival) confirms the 2026 dates. The [festival website](https://www.oakharborapplefestival.com/) says admission is free and a shuttle from the high school parking lot costs $2 per person.

Saturday's Paulette Weirich Memorial Grand Parade starts at 2 p.m. Its [2026 entry form](https://www.oakharborapplefestival.com/_files/ugd/867467_9a1748716e244f9cb193c7b3800ef9b6.pdf) gives the route: from Oak Harbor Junior High School south on Church Street, then east on Main Street to Veterans Park.

Sunday's [car, truck and motorcycle show](https://www.oakharborapplefestival.com/car-bike-show) runs from noon to 4 p.m. at Oak Harbor High School, 11661 W. State Route 163. Vehicle registration runs from 10 a.m. to noon; the organizer provides a separate registration link for exhibitors.

The [Apple Run](https://www.oakharborapplefestival.com/apple-run) returns Sunday with what organizers describe as its original road course. The one-mile children's fun run starts at 1:30 p.m., followed by the 5K at 2 p.m. The dated festival schedule places both races at 315 N. Church St. Runners should use the event's registration page for fees and entry details.

The festival also lists a children's activity area and more than 120 vendors. Free festival admission does not establish that rides, food or other activities are free.

## Erie County spotlights local addiction treatment and a 24-hour help line

Section: local · Community: Erie County · Byline: JD

Summary: A new county video highlights Kaptur Treatment & Recovery. The department lists detox, residential and outpatient care, with a phone contact available around the clock.

The Erie County Health Department is directing residents seeking substance-use treatment to Kaptur Treatment & Recovery through a new video and a phone contact available 24 hours a day.

The department's [Oct. 8 website post](https://eriehealthohio.com/2-minute-health-break-inside-kaptur-treatment-recovery/) promotes a two-minute video featuring recent graduates. The announcement itself is dated Oct. 7.

The post lists 567-304-3900 for confidential guidance around the clock. The department's separate [services page](https://eriehealthohio.com/kaptur-treatment-recovery-services/) lists 419-624-3353 to schedule a confidential intake.

According to that services page, the Erie County Detoxification Center has 16 beds for medically monitored withdrawal care. Kaptur's residential program has 14 beds and offers a 28-day program for adults with substance-use disorders and co-occurring mental health conditions.

The department also lists intensive outpatient care, treatment combining medication with counseling, and an outpatient detox program for patients assessed as medically appropriate. Those services do not all require a residential stay.

The announcement does not give current bed availability, waiting times or treatment prices. Residents should ask the intake team about admission requirements, insurance and costs. The video highlights existing services; it does not announce a new facility or an expansion.
